Porta — AirDrop. With links.

Porta turns your phone into a temporary personal sharing node. Pick a file, generate a live link, and stream it to anyone on any device. No uploads, no accounts, no waiting.

End-to-end encrypted (DTLS over WebRTC). No cloud uploads — files stay on your phone. Works in any browser — the receiver needs no app. Now in private beta.
